Our Story & Practitioners

About Smile Works

An independent dental practice in Poombuhar Nagar, Kolathur, dedicated to gentle pediatric dental care, conservative tooth preservation, and transparent doctor-patient relationships.

Practice Philosophy

Dentistry practiced with patience, not quotas.

Smile Works was established in Kolathur with a straightforward clinical purpose: to deliver calm, trustworthy dental care where patients feel fully informed, never rushed, and never pressured into treatments they don't require.

Unlike corporate dental chains that rotate unfamiliar visiting associates, Smile Works is owned and operated by resident clinicians. When you bring your child or come for a consultation, you are seen directly by Dr. J. Jaganesh, MDS or Dr. T.S. Divya, BDS from initial diagnostic examination through aftercare.

"Our motto, 'Dentistry Beyond Expectations', is our daily standard: taking the time to listen, numbing gently, explaining options clearly, and preserving healthy natural tooth structure whenever possible."

— Clinical Team, Smile Works

Key Practice Tenets

  • 01
    Direct Doctor Consistency: Every clinical examination and procedure is carried out personally by qualified doctors.
  • 02
    Pediatric Specialization: MDS-level training specifically dedicated to child behavior guidance, baby tooth preservation, and growth monitoring.
  • 03
    Conservative Dental Ethics: We prioritize remineralization, sealants, and minimal tooth preparation over aggressive drilling.
  • 04
    Neighborhood Accessibility: Based on 2nd Main Road in Poombuhar Nagar, serving Kolathur, Villivakkam, Perambur, and surrounding North Chennai communities.
