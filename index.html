About Smile Works

“Dentistry beyond expectations” is our promise of patience, clarity, and comfort.

Located in Poombuhar Nagar, Kolathur, Smile Works was established to provide North Chennai families with a calm, friendly, and scientifically grounded dental home. We believe visiting the dentist should be free from anxiety, pressure, or rushed recommendations.

Every patient receives an unhurried, one-on-one consultation. We explain your oral health conditions with complete transparency, discuss preventive alternatives first, and ensure you are in full control of your care plan.
