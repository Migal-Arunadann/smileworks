Dental Treatments & Clinical Services

From gentle pediatric dentistry for young children to root canals and conservative restorative care for adults, all procedures are performed directly by qualified doctors in Kolathur.

Featured Specialization Lead: Dr. J. Jaganesh, MDS (Pediatric Dentist)

Pediatric & Preventive Dentistry for Children

Children require specialized clinical patience, gentle psychological handling, and pediatric dental expertise. At Smile Works, Dr. J. Jaganesh provides focused pediatric care from infancy through adolescence, ensuring fear-free visits and healthy smile development.

✓
Early Dental Examinations (From Age 1): Assessing oral milestone development, eruption sequencing, and identifying early feeding-related cavity risks before they progress.
✓
Child Behavior Management & Desensitization: Tell-Show-Do techniques, relaxed operatory pacing, and no-fear communication to eliminate lifelong dental anxiety.
✓
Fluoride Varnish & Mineral Defense: Topical application of high-potency fluoride to remineralize weak enamel and protect young teeth against acid erosion.
✓
Pit & Fissure Sealants: Protective coatings placed over deep molar grooves to physically shield cavity-prone chewing surfaces from food stagnation.
✓
Space Maintainers & Habit Appliances: Custom fixed or removable appliances to prevent teeth shifting after early baby tooth loss and correct thumb-sucking habits.
✓
Pediatric Tooth Restorations & Pulpectomies: Gentle, child-appropriate nerve care (baby tooth root canal) and pediatric tooth-colored crowns to preserve natural teeth.

Conservative & Tooth-Colored Fillings

Minimally invasive composite resin restorations designed to blend imperceptibly with your natural tooth structure. We remove only the decayed enamel, preserving healthy natural dentin to ensure long-term structural tooth strength.

Composite bonding · Shade matching · Mercury-free restorations
03

Root Canal Therapy (Endodontics)

When deep decay or trauma inflames the inner dental pulp, root canal treatment removes infection, cleanses the canal system, and seals the tooth safely. We prioritize patient comfort and conservative preservation of your natural tooth roots.

Pain relief · Infection resolution · Rotary canal instrumentation
04

Growth-Phase Orthodontic Guidance

Early assessment of jaw growth, tooth spacing, and bite alignment in growing children. Interceptive measures such as space maintainers and habit-breaking appliances guide permanent teeth into healthy alignment and minimize future complex treatments.

Space maintainers · Habit correction · Early growth assessment
05

Ultrasonic Scaling & Periodontal Care

Gentle ultrasonic cleaning, tartar removal, and gum maintenance to eliminate inflammation, prevent bleeding gums, protect alveolar bone support, and restore oral freshness.

Tartar removal · Enamel protection · Periodontal foundation defense
06

Diagnostic Checkups & Preventive Guidance

Comprehensive oral hygiene consultations, individualized brushing instructions, and dietary counseling to stop decay before it begins. Regular 6-month checkups keep your entire family out of the dental chair for complex procedures.

Comprehensive exam · Enamel tracking · Personalized home care plan
